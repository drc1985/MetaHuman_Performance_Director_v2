Empowering Filmmakers & Virtual Production

Eliminate multi-day curve editing and expensive mocap re-shoots with automated intent orchestration.

Natural Language Directing

Type or speak acting notes directly inside Unreal Engine. The system translates dramatic intent across face, cervical spine, gaze, and emotion channels instantly.

Non-Destructive Take System

Every take generates a dedicated Level Sequence asset. A/B compare baseline and alternate iterations instantly via the takes dropdown without altering speech lip-sync.

Selective Channel Preservation

Lock dialogue audio, speech phonemes, facial expressions, gaze, or body gestures. Preserve baseline lip-sync key-for-key while layering dramatic nuance.

RigLogic DNA Synthesis

Directly manipulates over 200 native MetaHuman facial DNA curves through 4 universal curve patterns: Pulse, Hold, Ramp, and Oscillation.

Body Micro-Behavior Library

Seamlessly layer conversational body language, shoulder tension, and cervical head turns with native Control Rig constraint blending.

In-Editor Voice Input

Press 'Hold to Talk' to speak acting notes directly to your digital actors using local, on-device voice transcription.

Two-Phase Directorial Pipeline

Built natively on top of Epic Games' first-party stack (MetaHuman Animator, RigLogic, and Sequencer).

  • Phase 1: Audio-to-Face Baseline
    Ingests dialogue audio (.wav) via MetaHuman Animator, locking phoneme speech timing into an isolated baseline sequence.
  • Phase 2: Intent-Driven Take Generation
    Parses natural language notes into structured JSON plans and bakes non-destructive Control Rig layers and expression curves.
